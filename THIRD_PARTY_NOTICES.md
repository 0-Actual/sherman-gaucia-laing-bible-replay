## World English Bible

The recovered historical web data identifies its 34-verse Creation Week `source_text` as the World English Bible (WEB). The publisher identifies the WEB text as public domain and identifies its name as a trademark used to distinguish faithful copies. See the publisher's [copyright notice](https://ebible.org/eng-web/copyright.htm) and [World English Bible site](https://worldenglish.bible/).

QEL's noncommercial restrictions do not apply to the public-domain WEB translation itself. Keep the edition label attached to unchanged source text. A transformed `calc_text`, sound mapping, numerical projection or QEL interpretation is a separate derived representation and must not be represented as an unchanged Bible translation.

## King James Version and source-language quotations

The expanded studies identify their English Scripture quotations as King James Version (KJV), distinct from the WEB text used by the historical web interface. Do not silently replace one edition with the other in a replay. Hebrew and Greek source spellings, transliterations, letter-value tables and interpretation are separately labeled in the studies and replay outputs.

The repository makes no ownership claim over these Scripture quotations. Any independently applicable source-text conditions remain in force. QEL attribution refers to the research direction, original study expression, selection, software and declared transformations to the extent those rights can be licensed.

## Included and absent assets

The selected historical source files retain their source labels. Unrecovered image assets are not represented as included or cleared for redistribution. A portable web adaptation, if supplied, labels its changes and omits unavailable illustrations. No complete SBL Greek New Testament, full Hebrew Torah corpus, proprietary model weights, or private interaction archive is distributed merely because an old source map refers to it.

Historical third-party notices are preserved where present. Any additional contribution must state its origin and applicable permissions before incorporation.
